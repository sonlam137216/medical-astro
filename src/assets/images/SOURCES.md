# Image sources

All files here were exported from the previous Melatec Figma file on 2026-10-04 at 2x and are **placeholders for the
final media library** (Cloudflare R2 + `media_assets`, Phase 5). They are the pictures used in the design, not
verified marketing assets.

Nguồn thiết kế chính từ ngày 10/10/2026: [Figma mới — Melatec](https://www.figma.com/design/9A1cfRPC1hncD00yh2t1BU/Untitled?node-id=0-1&p=f&t=BhkYqiP9QiBaOoOC-0).
Ảnh hiện có vẫn là bản xuất từ thiết kế cũ ngày 04/10/2026; chưa đối chiếu hoặc xuất lại từ file mới.
Cần kiểm tra lại ảnh, cách crop và logo khi cập nhật UI theo thiết kế mới.

- Photos are exports of the _rendered_ design layers (already cropped to the box used in Figma), converted to
  WebP. The transparent rounded corners that Figma bakes in were trimmed away.
- `logo.png` and `logo-white.png` are raster logos (370 px wide). Ask the owner for the vector original.
- `social/*.png` are the four social icons from the design.

Review with the owner before launch:

- `photos/before-after.webp`: shows a real patient, and the "BEFORE / AFTER" labels and slider handle are baked
  into the picture. Confirm written consent and usage rights, then rebuild it as a real comparison component.
- `photos/dentist-digital-scan.webp`: replaced on 2026-10-10 by `image 1` from the new Figma file (no baked-in text this time). Confirm it is a licensed or consented photo.
- `photos/home-hero.webp`: Home hero background (`image 19` of the new Figma file, 2000 px wide). Stock-looking photo, confirm the licence.
- `photos/hanoi-tower-wide.webp`: wide Hanoi Turtle Tower photo (`image 34` of the new Figma file, 2000 px wide) used for the navy page heroes (Services, Our Doctors, Packages). Confirm the licence.
- `photos/implant-hero.webp`: 3D illustration of an implant beside natural teeth (`image 35` of the new Figma file, 2000 px wide), hero of the Dental Implants page. Confirm the licence.
- `photos/panoramic-xray.webp`: a panoramic X-ray. Confirm it is a licensed stock image or a consented case.
- `doctors/doctor-portrait.webp`: one portrait is reused for every doctor in the design.
- `photos/hotel-room.webp`: reused for all five "Inside Melatec" tiles in the design.
- `photos/vietnam-*.webp`, `da-nang.webp`: travel photography; confirm the license.
